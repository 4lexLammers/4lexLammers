## Dr. Alexander Lammers

**AI Transformation · Consultant · Lecturer · Researcher**

PhD economist working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, MLOps and organization. I lead AI transformations for DAX40 and German Mittelstand companies, from board-level strategy into reliable production, with a focus on regulated industries such as financial services and insurance. I lecture on AI and Business Analytics at the FOM University of Applied Sciences and publish peer-reviewed research on how technology reshapes work, organization and governance.

### What I work on

- **AI implementation & MLOps:** GenAI solutions, LLM agents, RAG and copilots from pilot to production, with data engineering and MLOps on Azure and AWS.
- **AI governance & EU AI Act readiness:** risk classification, model lifecycle and the documentation that holds up under regulation.
- **Research:** peer-reviewed work on AI, technological change and organizations.
- **Teaching:** M.Sc. modules on Data Engineering and MLOps at FOM; mentoring in the AI track at TechLabs Düsseldorf.

### Selected research
